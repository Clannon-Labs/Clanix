# Expert reports

Local, in-depth evidence from delegated experts. Reports are gitignored because
they are working context; durable conclusions belong in `comms/`, `PROJECT.md`,
or another tracked product document.

Store reports at `reports/<role>/YYYY-MM-DD_short-slug.md`:

```markdown
# Title

Date: YYYY-MM-DD
Role: runtime | server | experience | verification | architecture | review
Brief: <brief filename>
Status: complete | partial | blocked

## Scope
## Evidence
## Work performed
## Verification
## Findings and risks
## Handoff
```

Reports inform decisions. They do not request a ruling.
